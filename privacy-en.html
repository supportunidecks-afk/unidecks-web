Privacy Policy

How UniDecks handles your data — in full, in plain language.

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Applies to the UniDecks iOS app

In short

  • Your decks, cards and study progress live in your own iCloud. The developer cannot access them.
  • The app shows ads, but never while you study. On iPhone and iPad, ads come from Google AdMob; you decide in Google's consent dialog whether Google may use data for them. You can turn ads off with “Ad-free” or with your credits.
  • The developer does not sell data and never requests App Tracking Transparency (ATT) access.
  • Only what you deliberately share with the community becomes public.
  • Performance data is sent only after you explicitly opt in and never contains study content.

2. Data we process

2.1 Account and sign-in

Signing in is optional and available via Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. We store:

  • the user identifier issued by the provider (for Apple, the anonymous subject ID),
  • your display name,
  • your email address if you choose to share it (with Apple this may be an anonymous relay address),
  • a profile picture, if available.

Purpose: authentication, attributing the decks you share, showing your name in the community and on leaderboards. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Without an account you can use the app locally but cannot share content.

2.2 Your study content (private iCloud sync)

Decks, cards, folders, study progress, statistics and settings are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own Apple account so they stay in sync across your devices. This data is controlled by you and Apple; the developer of this app cannot technically access it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Apple's processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

2.3 Shared content (public community database)

When you share a deck, rate a deck, suggest a change or take part in the weekly leaderboard, the following is stored in a public CloudKit database and is visible to other users:

  • the shared deck's content (cards, subject, university, degree programme, optional professor) and its versions,
  • your display name, profile colour and profile picture if set,
  • ratings, votes, change proposals and reports you submit,
  • for the leaderboard: minutes studied, number of cards learned and viewed per week.

Purpose: operating the community features. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Sharing is always voluntary; decks you do not share stay private.

2.4 AI features (Google Gemini)

For optional features — generating and sorting cards, detecting subject categories, study plan suggestions — the required content is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing:

  • the text of the cards or decks involved,
  • documents or images you select (e.g. PDF lecture notes) when you generate cards from them.

This transfer only happens when you actively trigger such a feature. The provider is Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC; transfers to third countries may occur on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. See the Google Privacy Policy. Please do not upload material you would not want processed by an external service.

2.5 Image search (Pexels)

When you search for an image in the card editor, your search term is sent to the Pexels API to return results. No account or study data is transmitted.

2.6 Feedback and reports

When you send feedback or report a deck, we store the message, the selected reasons, a randomly generated installation ID and the app and device version. Providing a contact email is voluntary and only used to reply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (improving and moderating the app).

2.7 Performance telemetry (opt-in only)

After a one-time prompt — the default is off — the app can send anonymous performance reports, at most once per day and device: device model, iOS and app version, usage duration, number of sessions, CPU time, memory footprint, UI hangs and crash reports from Apple's MetricKit. No study content, card text or names are included. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You can withdraw it at any time under Profile → App settings → Performance.

2.8 Notifications

If you allow push notifications (e.g. study reminders), a device token is processed via Apple. You can revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

2.9 Advertising

UniDecks shows ads in “Explore” and after a study session, never while you study. New installations stay ad-free for the first seven days. Ads are always labelled “Ad”.

Sponsor ads: Ads from our own partners are chosen only by the content you are looking at (e.g. the subject of a deck). No profile is built and no data about you is shared with sponsors. The device stores only two values for this, which never leave it: the time of the first launch (for the ad-free start period) and a random number that keeps a small share of devices permanently ad-free.

Google AdMob (iPhone and iPad): If no sponsor ad is booked for a space, the app loads an ad via Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google processes in particular the IP address, device and app information (e.g. model, operating system, language), approximate location derived from the IP address, interactions with ads and diagnostic data. Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used because the app does not request tracking access.

Before any ad is loaded, the app shows Google's consent dialog in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, where you can choose “Consent” or “Do not consent” on equal terms. Without consent, Google shows only limited, non-personalised ads. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG (consent); for non-personalised ads Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (funding the free app). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Profile → Settings → Ads → “Privacy settings for ads”. Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Details: How Google uses data for advertising.

Turning ads off: Each ad space can be turned off with earned credits for 24 hours, one week or one month. This is stored with your credits in your private iCloud.

2.10 Purchases (“Ad-free”)

“Ad-free” is available as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase. Payment and billing are handled entirely by Apple (in-app purchase). The developer receives no payment data; the app only checks with Apple whether a purchase exists. You manage and cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3. What does not happen

  • No ads while studying, in the editor, in widgets or in notifications.
  • The app never requests App Tracking Transparency access and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No sale of data. Study content, card text and profile details are never given to advertising partners.
  • No access to contacts, location or health data.

5. Retention

  • Private study data: until you delete it or remove the app data from your iCloud.
  • Shared decks and profile data: until you unshare the deck or delete your account.
  • Feedback and reports: up to 24 months for handling and abuse prevention.
  • Performance reports: up to 12 months, then deleted automatically.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR), and you may withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → App settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, your shared decks, ratings, leaderboard entries and the link to Apple or Google. Alternatively, email Support.unidecks@gmail.com; deletion happens within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is aimed at students aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect data from younger children.

8. Security

All transfers are encrypted (TLS). Sign-in credentials are stored in the device keychain.

9. Changes

This policy is updated when app features change. The current version is always available on this page.
