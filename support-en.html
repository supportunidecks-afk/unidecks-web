Frequently asked questions

Does UniDecks cost anything?

No. Every feature is free — no paywall, no card limit. In return the app shows ads, never while you study. If you prefer no ads, turn them off for a while with credits or buy “Ad-free” (monthly, yearly or one-time).

Where are my cards stored?

In your own iCloud. Decks sync automatically between iPhone, iPad and Mac as long as you are signed in with the same Apple Account and iCloud Drive is enabled.

My decks don't show up on my second device

Check: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Drive enabled, the same Apple Account on both devices, and a working internet connection. Then open Profile → Sync in the app to check the status and trigger a sync.

How do I share a deck with the community?

Open the deck → deck settings → Share with community. You set subject, university, degree programme and optionally the professor so others can find it.

How do I report problematic content?

Open the community deck → menu in the top right → "Report deck" → choose a reason. Reports are reviewed and violating content is removed within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → App settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your profile, shared decks, ratings and leaderboard entries. Alternatively email Support.unidecks@gmail.com — deletion happens within 30 days.

Which import formats are supported?

Common flashcard exports (text, CSV, common deck packages) as well as PDFs and images you can turn into cards.

Can I study with a game controller?

Yes. Pair a Bluetooth gamepad in iOS Settings; the app detects it automatically and lets you run a study session hands-free.

Found a bug?

Tell us what happened, what you expected, and which device and iOS version you use. A screenshot helps a lot.
